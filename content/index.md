PRE-HUMAN is a story written by Isa/Ducko and predecessor to POST-HUMAN. 

The story follows Amir, who woke up in a desert village with seemingly no will of his own, he eventually is joined by siblings [[Azhar]] and Sarah to figure out his origin while they learn more about the world.
