Sarah is the daughter and second child of Salman and Fida Hajar. She is [[Azhar Hajar]] 's younger brother who she drags along with her onto the ship that starts the story, meeting and naming [[Nikhil Nicolas|Amir]] and eventually becoming a [[Patron#Marks|mark]] of [[Ith]]

Sarah is very double-faced, being extremely soft and kind on the surface, but selfish and irritable on the inside. This is due to responsibility and position as potential concubine, to improve their village's standing but having to sacrifice her own agency and desires.

She eventually makes the impulsive decision to run away on a ship, Azhar following her, which kickstarts the whole story.

## Appearance

Sarah is a bit over average height, being only a miniscule amount taller than Amir, fueling her excuse to be an older sister. She has beautiful wavy, long hair with curtain bangs, all with a purple-ish gradient tint, though she quickly replaces this with an average length ponytail and layered bangs. Unlike like Azhar, her best feature ends up being her eyes, being properly purple and vibrant, and no mean-face as her eyes aren't sharp. Her face looking more innocent and bubbly as well, which is ruined by her personality and expressions when revealed.

Body wise, Sarah is on the thin and slim side, as she hasn't had to do much physical labor, leading to her having trouble keeping up with Azhar and Amir at the start of the journey, though eventually properly pulling her own weight.

She often changes looks, being very happy as she can finally look the way she wants. While her top often changes, she prefers baggy pants and often wears a sirwal, with her preference been on the more cool-ish side, though she actually has less accessories than Azhar, who has multiple rings.

## Personality

Sarah is very double faced and has facades, on the surface she's incredibly kind and forgiving with her demeanor being like an angel. This is because of her responsibility towards her [[Aintidina|village]] who didn't have a good standing in the eyes of the monarchy. She acts as a very soft, kind, positive and nearly flawless girl, though stay believable enough for the only ones that end up know what she's actually like to be [[Azhar Hajar|Azhar]] and [[Samir Hidayat|Samir]].

In actuality, Sarah is fairly selfish, brash, passionate, feisty, childish when possible, greedy and is not afraid to take all advantages for herself, especially so with those of higher standing who can easily afford losses and have more than just opportunities taken from them, which is a far-cry from her façade. Due to her earlier explained façade, she is extremely good at reading the room and giving people what they want, on their journey she mainly uses this for information gathering, haggling, negotiations and gambling, which doesn't always go right. Her façade quickly disappears though, as there's no more need for it. 

Though in the end Sarah is still kind and very energetic, while not empathetic like Azhar, she is still sympathetic. This is something she's even a bit insecure about, looking up up to her brother even if she thinks he's stupid in some ways. She only really cares about a select few close to her, who she prioritizes above all else, her love is given differently depending on the person though, Azhar often getting lectured and bullied a bit, while Amir gets treated like a baby brother she gets to dote on. Throughout the story she becomes more open and appreciative, being the one that wanted to travel most, and becoming very expressive even with body language the moment it properly sinks in that they're living their own lives.

## Background

Being born as second child, girl and a whole 4 years after her brother, she grew up slightly spoiled. Sarah used to cling to people a lot, always wanting to do something, her father and uncle often took her to the [[Alsahriqa|capital]] which is how she got her knowledge of bartering and trade. Though the one that had to deal with her most was her brother Azhar who she really liked.

Unlike Azhar, Sarah had less trouble making friends, being very popular among others her age and even making friends in the capital, whenever she wasn't around her parents or brother, she was with her friends. Though one time when in [[Alsahriqa]], the son of a Quarter Vizier took interest in her, wanting to take her as concubine in the future, this opportunity would allow her village to have more stability and a higher standing. This was the turning point where Sarah had to stop being herself and her privacy was taken away, having to always be around people unless at home. Her desires and personality eventually translating into the power of full control, being status and wealth.

This is how Sarah became who she is, being saint-like as she should be, being purely kind instead of kind and cheeky, to the point of her saying that while she's slightly anxious, she's sure to love and be happy soon enough. While her actual feelings were very much vented onto Azhar who was the closest person, and Samir, who seemed to be the person closest to Azhar. Due to this she felt that her connection with her friends grew less sincere, even if she was still around them.

## Relationships
#### [[Azhar Hajar]]
Azhar is her older brother who reluctantly joins Sarah onto the ship and starts travelling with her and Amir. Generally it seems that they just put up with each other, which is mostly true, Azhar cares and does understand his sister, but doesn't like her selfishness and general behavior. Though he does indulge her, often getting in trouble for taking her to the capital when she asks, this being a form of escapism for her. 

When young they were way sweeter, Sarah often tried to get Azhar to indulge her, asking to play and for food and such, him not minding and being happy to most of the time. They grew slightly apart when Sarah grew up enough and started hanging out with others, being happy if Azhar would join but him being busy and her not thinking much of it as that's how he's always been. Their relationship turned into Azhar just listening as Sarah told him to be less passive, especially after her future was set in stone.

Azhar eventually comes around later in the story, properly understanding what she meant as he's never really had any will to act for himself at a larger scale. He becomes more active due to both her and Amir, going as far to even lecture Sarah back, which turns into them bickering, this often being about Sarah not being as thoughtful as he'd like. In the end he still puts Sarah at the top of his priorities, going as far as permanently losing an eye while protecting her in [[Fiorente]]
#### [[Nikhil Nicolas]]
Amir was the name given to him by Sarah, naming him a name that meant 'prince' as she loves status, and also cause Samir was around which means that name was floating around in her head. Amir turns into a little brother figure for Sarah, this being her second form of control and agency after running away in the first place, but it's mostly Sarah acting like it as Amir couldn't communicate and barely reacted to her antics.

Eventually Amir gets treated more like a baby or animal, which is natural but is something she grows conscious of and dislikes the less animal-like Amir becomes. Sarah grows to actually care about Amir, becoming especially doting and protective, him becoming a reason for her wanting to better herself and her selfishness, this does good and is mostly why Amir eventually becomes fairly expressive, innocent and bubbly. He does see Azhar and Sarah as his older siblings and is appreciative,

During the events of [[Fiorente]], Sarah is most affected both with Amir's disappearance, stressing to go look for him, and his eventual loss, where she is extremely reluctant to interact with Nikhil in any way. Her feelings are generally very complicated, with a sense of guilt, responsibility, sadness and hate, refusing to call him anything but Amir when she does have to mention him. She eventually gives in after a fit, calling him Niko but never really apologizing, she does grow to care for him as much as she did and more.
#### [[Samir Hidayat]]
The connection between Sarah and Samir was mostly fueled by Samir. He was never specifically close with her unlike Azhar who had his troubles from birth, giving him more attention and Sarah mostly just being jealous of them spending time together and forcing herself between them when she could.

It's only after Samir returns from a trip and learns of Sarah's position that he starts becoming a proper pillar for her to lean on, but sadly he wasn't able to do much in the face of higher powers, being a simple man. Samir, while worrying about Sarah's personality and the trouble it could cause, deeply respects her ambition and sense of self, thinking Azhar could use a bit of that. While their connection isn't as deep as the one between Samir and Azhar, she still got similar treatment, often being played favorites with compared to other kids in the village.
#### [[Devi Vincine]]
At first Devi's opinion on Sarah was 'a bit worrisome' at first, due to learning of her position as someone's, being Amir's, main influence, though this quickly faded into respecting her more, being happy to listen to her rambles and entertain her here and there during the sibling's time at [[The Branched Castle]], thinking she brightens the room. Though just like Azhar, she only starts caring about Sarah later in the story and only being happy that Nikhil was being taken care of at first.

After learning from Devi that Amir was older than her, with her deflating, she quickly jumped to having Devi as the childhood friend of her 'supposed-to-be' younger brother, replace him as younger sister, but deflated again after Devi mentioned that she was older than Amir which was on purpose. In the end Devi does grow a little jealous of Sarah's position and does whatever she can to poke fun at her using her 'superior' position with him. When alone they just talk like normal friends though, with Devi eventually also caring about her as Sarah cares about her.

## Equipment & Abilities

Due to Sarah's lack of activity and labor growing up, she's the most out of shape at the start of the story, often tripping, getting stuck and being exhausted, making Azhar carry a portion of her luggage as well. Even though she catches up eventually, especially stamina wise, her strength still lies in her social skills which the party relies on, with her taking the lead whenever dealing with people and in civilization.

She's very talented in reading the room and knowing what people want, which she uses to her advantage when winning favor and haggling. In combat she initially has a Telek, given to her by Azhar as she had to have a weapon, but she eventually gets taught how to use polearms by Azhar and switches to a Ji polearm herself.

She mostly uses her polearm with in a similar way to Azhar's controlling, flinging way. Sarah mainly relies on distorting her targets sense of balance and using push and pull to topple them, she also instead afraid to let go of her weapon and go for cheap shots.
#### [[Charqye|Charqü]] 
While Sarah has a similar opinion on being a living vessel as Azhar at first, she eventually becomes a vessel for a charqü that lets her distort other's senses. Initially she doesn't understand how to use it since its an abstract ability. She eventually mostly uses it to throw her targets sense of balance and distorting vision with blur, dizzying her target, but eventually leans into proper illusions, which she also uses for gambling.
