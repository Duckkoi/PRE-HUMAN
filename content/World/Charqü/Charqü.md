Charqü *(pronounced sharply Char kye)* are the most basic form of existence and can be considered the 'potential' for it at all. At first they're simply nothing, again purely being potential, overtime they integrate into their surroundings, mimicking its  information, meaning smell, sounds, behavior, colors, shape, and even thoughts which all dictate how it's perceived, though as they don't start out very accurate it can make them seem to be supernatural, this includes them experimenting in a way when active. While this behavior suggests that they're sentient, they  strictly are not, except for some very distinct special cases.

> Examples of this are unnaturally hollow mountains, trees growing from the ceiling, roots coming out of the cliff side holding up chunks of land, an ocean floating above an ocean, water flowing upstream, general illusions or rules being in effect such as one way entry.

Charqü growth doesn't come in stages and is fairly linear until they fully integrate, meaning they've become a part of the world and can only refine further, but even this single stage doesn't last long. When young they have a lot of interactive freedom, being able to mimic many properties, but have little energy to be very active unlike older Charqü who are generally more specialized and limited, but have more energy in return for their age, the latter being more active and thus more 'supernatural' on their own. There's no one path for their destination, as long as it's within their scope, which keeps getting smaller.

They are born from the Cradle, at center of the world and return there once they finish their growth cycle. Even though they're born in the Cradle, they quickly make their way to rootwells (commonly called faraway-wells), nodes that have formed due to the large amount of charqü at a suitable location, due to this nature the area around said rootwells is very active, even if its mostly young charqü, the amount makes up for their individual lack of energy.

Spirits are the old name for charqü

Spirits are the most basic form of being and existence, but to otherse they look like the supernatural, they cause things such as waterfalls that flow up and grass sprouts that are too big.  They also cause a lot of  weird shapes in nature, such as crescent mountain or donut shaped flying islands.  The more spirits there are in an area the weirder the happenings can be and the more often they appear. Spirits do not have a set shape, size or color, though they are all weightless. All they do is float around, some are more curious or cautious than others, but they tend to stay away from humans, note that this does not mean that there are none, just a lower concentration. Due to large concentrations of spirits being rare near humans, the terrain around civilization is very normal, unlike the very deep wild where "anything" could happen.

(I recommend still reading everything even if its not underscored, since this is a whole new system that doesn't exist in life or media and has major effects in the story and world.)

When given time, spirits start change and gain properties and in turn according to their environment, this is also the reason they stay away from humans, which influence their growth too much. If a spirit is in the middle of an ocean, it could gain the properties of, and in turn "abilities" like turning into water, creating salt or even shooting turtle shells. Though, this as well is dependent on growth, spirits start with infinite freedom, they can interact with light, gravity, plants, ions, blood, everything in their vicinity, but it can't control it, due to it having no stamina. Older spirits are the opposite, they can't interact with as much and are more specialized, but they can properly interact with their properties and utilize them. (E.g. Spirit at sea can interact with the sky, light, gravity, the sea, etc.

Spirits are able to inhabit objects, animals and humans. When a spirit inhabits one of these, it does not gain the ability to control its body. There are a few rules for a spirit inhabiting objects, animals and humans, plants fall under objects.

1. If a spirit inhabits an object: It can be used, but it can't do anything by itself.
2. If a spirit inhabits an animal: The animal could learn how to control it through instinct. But it also has the change to ignore it.
3. If a spirit inhabits a human: The human will gain its abilities, though it must be practiced just like writing and walking.

Spirits are 'born' from the center of the world. They 'die' after specializing so long that they become 'it', for example a blade of grass and eventually returning to the center of the world.

Just like any other creature, spirits have stamina. When its stamina gets fully expended it converts into a blighted spirit. Unlike spirits, blighted spirits do not have a special ability and are all generally the same, when a spirit changes into a blighted spirit, it loses its previous ability and becomes sensitive to its surroundings and becomes completely adaptive, greatly changing its surroundings, cause of this they can even learn human language, though this is rare.

Since they are still spirits they still have the ability to make supernatural things happen, and if they become intelligent they could even control it. There are again a few rules for a blighted spirit possessing objects, animals and humans.

1. If a spirit possessing an object becomes a blighted spirit: The object breaks or rots with the blighted spirit leaving it
2. If a spirit possessing an animal becomes a blighted spirit: The blighted spirit leaves the body, leaving the animal to die or morph / The animal now more survivability replacing the old abilities.
3. If a spirit possessing a human becomes a blighted spirit: The blighted leave the body, leaving the human or dead or with paralyzed limbs and organ failure or anything similar / The human now has more survivability replacing the old abilities.

(see examples of blighted creatures)

The new abilities or 'talents' a blighted spirit has when possessing a human or animal exist to preserve the body. They all have the aptitude to learn skills such as nullifying poisons, regenerating which could even lead to leaving the host immortal, though they all excel in different areas. They still have an impact on supernatural events. Just like spirits, the abilities of blighted spirits can be controlled, which means that regenerating on command is possible which is called over-regenerating.

Over-regenerating can lead to transmutating the body with possibilities such as growing nails, hardening the body and even extending outside the body. Side effects when regenerating are heating up, nausea, tardiness, nosebleeds and sensitivity.

Blighted spirits can not 'die' in the same way as spirits as they do not grow in the ordinary way. Destroying or killing whatever it's inhabiting does 'kill' it though, blighted spirits are essentially weakened.

To follow up on the earlier mention of spirits inhabiting objects, there is more to it. Besides people being able to use spirits by being inhabited by hem, both spirits and blighted spirits, some can also interact with them even while uninhabited. Generally the only people that can do this are ones that are either heavily focused, simple minded, straightforward and such, cause they can present clear goals for spirits to follow. Otherwise they would follow the same rules as other spirits, keeping clear from humans as they dilute their growth too much.

There are four major categories that people fall in, though they all generally share qualities even if they are specialized.

1. Nurturers: Control and moniter the growth of spirits,
2. Tinkerers: Inhabits the spirit in tools and gets it to react to actions taken with the tool, for example a trigger being pulled
3. Guides: Controls the actions of a spirit, increasing and decreasing its activity and herding them
4. Scribes: Written and vocal commands, similar to tinkerers, but the process is very different
