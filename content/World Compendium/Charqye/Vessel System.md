This is the counterpart to the uninhabited method and tool-vessel methods used by [[Handlers]].
A vessel refers to anything inhabited by [[content/World Compendium/Charqye/index|charqye]], with the ones of note being living vessels and vessel-tools, which are plant or object as vessels turned into tools created by [[Handlers#Artificers|artificers]], allowing for a way to use charqye with less risk of being affected by blight when overusing the charqye, but with less intuitiveness and no flexibility.

[[content/World Compendium/Charqye/index|Charqye]] stay with their vessel until it's in a recognizable state, living vessels are able to control the charqye they are inhabited by, with humans, even with charqye being averse, naturally having a lot more potential than animals due to intelligence, curiosity and ingenuity. For them there's many ways to use charqye, one related to clouds allowing them to control not only that, water and lightning too. They are also prosper with the charqye, building up its energy and building habits with triggers and being able to control the scope of their charqye, trading flexibility for energy and stability.

> Commonly used triggers include vocals, gestures and stanches. These help perform more complicated actions, you can view them as macro's.
> 
> !WIP! The scope can be increased by absorbing other powerful charqye, the more similar the better, though this is very difficult due to low odds of discovery. 

## Blighted charqye

Being the vessel of a blighted charqye is fairly straightforward, the vessel benefits from a passive form of regeneration derived their singular trait of 'being', remaining as they are. This goes for objects too, but as there's no natural healing its less effective. On the other end you have active usage which is generally enhancing enhancing and forcefully 'being more' which means over-regenerating, the application varies widely though, some being able to mimic others or being able to clone themselves, though these are al temporary as it's not themselves anymore.

> This does not mean that they halt aging in any way as aging is the natural state life is in, this also includes chronic health conditions, the only way to cure these is to learn how to control said blighted charqye and curing yourself while keeping it dormant can be very taxing.

Vessels of blighted charqye are again completely different from blighted creatures, the former strictly being vessels, not necessarily affected by blight and the latter being creatures affected by blight, often to the extent that they can be considers monstrous.

It's possible to be a vessel for multiple charqye, though the more are housed, the more unstable it is with the risk of blighting going up. This goes for both normal and blighted charqye.
