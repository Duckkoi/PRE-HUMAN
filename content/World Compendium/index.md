The world is split into two major ideas, the known world and [[The Wilds]]. They're split mainly due to the positive feedback loop between humans and [[World Compendium/Charqye/index|charqye]], being humans being uncomfortable in unknown and dangerous wilds and charqye naturally being deterred by consciousness as information, which causes natural borders around civilization. 

Humanity functions as multiple regions of which most only vaguely, or don't at all know about the world outside of their own region and can be though of similarly to how the world worked before the Age of Discovery, with the world of the story entering a similar era.

## General

Due to the [[Priming]], wiping civilizations, and the natural wall that is [[The Wilds]], human civilizations exist in isolated pockets, each with their own history, culture and technology, though they all share the same language as a rule created long ago. The technology is generally around the same level, similar to the Renaissance era, though this can heavily deviate to lower technology and even higher such as diesel/steampunk with civilizations wielding the latter technology often being the ones that survived the priming or are being influenced by a powerful [[Patron]].
