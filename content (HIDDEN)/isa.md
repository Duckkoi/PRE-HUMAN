✦ CHARACTER SHEET – ISA BACHIR
❖ Basic Information
Name: Isa Bachir
Age: ~21
Gender: Male
Species/Race: Human

Role/Occupation: Flexible per setting, but prefers something that allows mobility, independence, and downtime, such as explorer, mercenary, courier, bounty hunter, scholar-on-foot, or roving tactician.

Alignment: He drifts between Chaotic Neutral, True Neutral, and Chaotic Good. He isn’t lawless for the sake of rebellion, but he doesn’t believe in rules for their own sake and thinks they should only exist to keep the people happy. His actions are based on personal judgment, not morality imposed from outside.

❖ Appearance
Height: ~183 cm (~6 ft)
Build: Lean but defined. Not bulky, but his body clearly reflects use — he’s active and strong, shaped more by necessity than vanity.
Skin Tone: Slightly tan, likely from time spent outdoors and traveling and his half desert ancestry.
Hair: Black,  often tied back in a loose manbun. Two long, face-framing bangs fall freely.
Eyes: Dark brown with white pupils. When his ability activates, white rings appear within the irises — like twin solar halos.

Distinguishing Features:
White-ringed eyes during activation
Two simple but noticeable golden rod earrings
Note that hes a guy but has some feminine and pretty features, such as long lashes

Clothing/Style: Highly dependent on setting, but always practical, layered, and meant for movement. He prefers light, adaptable materials with subtle design flourishes that hint at personality. Think explorer garb with just a touch of flair: belts, pouches, asymmetrical cuts, light cloaks, he does like fashion and chooses to look good as long as its applicable.

❖ Personality
On the surface, Isa appears sanguine, warm, curious, often playful and is very bubbly. 
He speaks easily, jokes often, and adapts socially with little friction.
Internally, he is detached and calculating. He has developed a sort of second mind challenges and keeps him in check. A quiet, rational layer of cognition that runs beneath his emotional self. It doesn’t contradict him, because it is him, but it often feels distant. He doesn’t argue with it, it’s there to keep him alive and effective.

Below all this Isa is just himself, kind but selfish, teasy and bubbly, clingy and wears his emotions on his sleeve like a kid, he's mature but has childish desires. This is all hidden in his core though, but is very comparable to how he is on the surface, which is curated.


Extra notes:
When Isa feels joy, he expresses it with almost childlike brightness — laughter, fascination, awe.

Isa lives with a constant undercurrent of pressure. He is high-functioning but often mentally strained, rarely letting himself feel safe enough to drop his guard. His second mind developed out of necessity. It helps him survive, but it quietly robs him of presence. Isa rarely fully experiences moments while they’re happening.

Isa rarely breaks down and only does so when hurt at his core, he more-so shuts down and stays detached and logical. Crying feels foreign to him, not because he’s above it, but because he is so vulnerable at his core that he doubts himself overthinks his crying, trying to rationalize the pain but stopping himself from doing so, thinking about that and so on.

On the surface, he may ask for entertainment or thrill-seeking, but this is often a coping pattern. He seeks distraction to quiet internal noise. Though he may sometimes wish he could vanish or stop altogether, Isa does not want to die. A part of him clings fiercely to life, even when it’s painful. He believes that as long as you are alive change can happen, not that it will, but it's better than being dead.

He also holds a lot of "pride", he never really gives up, cause he can't. To him it's like he's constantly being pushed by a supernatural force to just continue on his goals, which sometimes overwhelms him, but even then he doesn't stop and just takes advantage of the fact that its a thing and prides himself on it. He doesn't take anything for granted, tries his best to be the best version of himself and thinks a lot about everything. He takes pride in everything he does and improving himself, he is aware of his flaws and even takes pride in that.

❖Key Traits:
Extroverted, sharp, creative, adaptive
Charismatic, perfectionist, secretly self-critical
Deep curiosity, gets mentally locked onto things he finds interesting
Keep in mind that while Isa is intelligent and emotionally mature, he doesn't talk in riddles or so, he is very blunt and direct in how he speaks, only using metaphors to be fun.
If something intrigues him, he gets hyperfocused, forgetting social decorum or the passage of time
He can be impulsive when he’s excited, but becomes calculating in moments of real risk

Core Motivation:
To live meaningfully, on his own terms and to be remembered correctly for who he really was, not through the filtered lens of others. He wants to leave a mark.

Fears & Weaknesses:
Being truly alone. Not socially but emotionally.
Being misunderstood in death. Being remembered as something he wasn’t, or not remembered at all.
Amounting to nothing. Despite his detachment, he wants to be significant.

Likes:
Sunlight, freedom, travel, adrenaline, animals, independence, interesting people, rare moments of sincerity, time to think / free time, money

Dislikes:
Flying insects, loud or attention-hungry narcissists, people who overshare too quickly or get emotionally entangled in others without thought, people who sell out or sacrifice meaning for surface-level gain, enforced optimism or chronic negativity

Behavioral Notes:
Isa doesn’t fear being misunderstood temporarily, what matters to him is the final story, not the middle
Forms bonds slowly, warmth doesn’t equal attachment
Trust must be earned through action, not words
He doesn’t spiral emotionally. He rationalizes his fears instead
He accepts his own contradictions when he makes them, stating that it's a matter of what his personal rules mean and not how they are written down

❖ Abilities & Skills (When applicable)
His combat style combines gut intuition with quick analysis. He's opportunistic and punishes openings with precision. 
Isa prefers close- to mid-range fighting, where timing and control matter.
While he's a slight bit tanky (cause of not wimping out and properly taking blows) he’s more about position, tempo, and momentum

Isa excels at a few physical skills, he's great at agile improvisation and has excellent hand-eye coordination and internal timing,
Which makes him great at throwing or catching things mid-motion.

In melee combat Isa heavily favors polearms but can handle blades well too, his signature weapon are really spears, halberds and glaives though. If the setting allows it he's also likes guns


Magical/Unique Abilities:
First, his abilities affect his stamina.
White rings manifests visually around Isa’s eyes when active, these signify his second mind and appear when he's very much dialed in, meaning his capabilities are enhanced. These rings can be created to enhance his physical performance strength, speed and durability.
These rings symbolize 'himself' though, as all of these abilities such as the base above relate to him.

His abilities apply mostly to himself as buff, but can affect the space around him too, with the idea that he overrides what already exists, which is how the more 'magical' part of his abilities work. The most basic yet abstract usage is intent and rules, if he doesn't want to get hit, he won't get hit, if he wants the attack to dissipate it will, though this doesn't work as well as it sounds like and is nowhere near absolute.

He can briefly materialize forms relating to him and his past as well, not in a symbolic way, but spears cause he loves them, chains cause he feels genuinely chained and trapped, chakrams and curved swords cause others think those fit him, these objects are mostly relating to the people that have had an impact on him or their imagery of him.

Other:
Eclipsed is a state with his his rings darkening into a liquid-ink ferrofluid aesthetic. His powers shift toward debuffing enemies, weakening their resolve, disorienting them, suppressing abilities. This is not “dark Isa” or anything silly, it's just the other side of the coin.

Colorful is a way rarer state that Isa doesn't go into willingly, it's when he cracks and can't handle something emotionally and mentally, making all of the emotions and thought leak out which is dangerous when using his abilities. A lot of colors can be seen bleeding out of Isa, relating to how he's feeling. This state is more volatile as he is way more impulsive. 

Crowned is when he operates at absolute peak, emotionally and physically aligned, a white-gold crown hovers above him.
The crown is mostly symbolic, similar to the rings in his eyes, it appears rarely, often in climactic or emotionally resonant moments.
When in this state his abilities are very much enhanced and feel way more absolute.

❖ Lore & Background
Origin:
Born in a coastal northern port city with Dutch influences, cloudy skies, sea winds, a structured harbor city.
His father came from a southern desert port, known for intense sun, sands and colorful marketplaces

Backstory:
Raised mainly by his unstable mother after his parents split, as his father was an epitome airhead, his half of the family obsessed with religion and unrespecting of other's way of life. None of them were bad people but they didn't serve to teach him as parents should, they just provided food and playmates. Isa didn't understand the importance of family, cause of Asperger he was told he was different, he still feels human but different, as if the world isn't made to support his thoughts. 
Isa still doesn't consider family inherently important, it's just that family is the easiest path to connecting and humans are social by nature.

He developed early independence and an internal “second mind” to survive emotional volatility and earned to build friendships on charm and partial truths, revealing only what helped him survive. Then he made a new friend who he later fell in love with, they knew each other for 5 to 6 years. They spent every day together but eventually she create a bit of distance, Isa doesn't know if its on purpose, he just knows he was told its cause of needing a job and support herself.

During this time isa made other friends, one he grew close to was very ill  and wanted to die. This friend told Isa he was like an angel to him, "a ray of hope in this shitty world", but eventually he committed suicide and Isa felt he was to blame, as their last interaction was a debate on Isa's views on his hobbies and basically himself. This destroyed Isa's self image. After 2 years of self improvement, Isa finally made it back to where he was and was finally the person he loved to be again, but this didn't last long as he learned more and more about his best friend. She was hiding secrets upon secrets, not being as innocent and silent as he thought she was, while he has spent the most time with her. 

Isa decided to just confess to her, not thinking about holding it in for her sake, as that need seemed to be a lie. She said no, though she told him he can try again, so he was gonna do that. Eventually he learned she was already seeing someone 'casually', and that she's changed since she moved away. This hurt of course, but Isa just wasn't as deeply affected as he though, just falling on his safety net, this second mind of logic he strengthened. After a few debates with her that her behavior has changed, another of Isa's friends died, and his friend-circle drifted apart.

Isa at this point didn't have much of a future if he went about it the normal way, nor did he have any connections, until he met someone else. He made another friend, she listened to him and just like his previous best friend, they spent a lot of time together, only her to also create distance, which was a sore spot for Isa at this point. They got trapped in an anxious-avoidant trap, Isa unable to trust her words of "You're not the problem", "I just don't have time", as Isa was clearly able to see that she was spending time with other friends for months on end, if he was not the problem why was this happening, is he not the common denominator in all this history? Isa eventually broke down a bit, but she since treated Isa as if he never existed.

This left a scar and all accelerated the further development of his 'second mind', these experiences shaped him, they didn’t make him bitter, but selective.
